In this study you will read about a medical bill and then contact {{ cp_contact }} to try to lower the amount you owe.

{{ if tell_negotiable }}
Hospitals do allow patients to ask for a lower amount, and this bill can be negotiated.
{{ endif }}
How it works
  • The hospital will tell you what you owe. You can accept the amount, or suggest a lower amount in dollars.
  • The hospital will reply to each suggestion. There are a limited number of exchanges, and the hospital will say when it is making its final offer.
  • When the hospital makes its final offer, you can accept it or decline it.
Your payment
  • You will receive your base payment of {{ base_pay }}.
  • You will also earn a bonus of {{ bonus_cents }} cents for every percentage point you save off the original bill, up to {{ bonus_cap }}. For example, saving 25% of the bill earns {{ example_bonus }}.
  • If you do not reach an agreement, you pay the full bill and earn no bonus.
